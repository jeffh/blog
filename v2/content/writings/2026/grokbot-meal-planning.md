Grokbot provides a series of different bot delegations that allow you to
delegate different tasks. I was using Dr Egg Bot from the Grokbot team to
generate bots. I ended up making two different kinds of bots: one meal prepper
and one for each shopping platform needed to do the shopping.

I gave it a basic task: generate a meal plan for me to prep and order. The goal
is to have it process and do all the shopping and ordering with my approval. It
was convenient to enter my login credentials securely for the agents to
use to shop on Amazon and Weee!.

The problem is that some of the foods were missing from the cart. It seems like
Grokbot doesn't fully accomplish the task and may have checked out on some of
these. It was very useful to get a high-level overview of what it was going to
do and what the plan was, including the bulk cooking breakdown and ordering to
help optimize things. Asking for extra clarification about how to prep was very
helpful. When producing the meals, it does seem to assume you have some cooking
experience and can double-check its work before it starts long, more
complicated, drawn-out tasks.
